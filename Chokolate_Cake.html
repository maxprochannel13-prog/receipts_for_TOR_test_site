  • Cornmeal mush

    • Ingredients for 8 servings:

    • 1 cup fine cornmeal
    • 4.5 cups water
    • 0.5 teaspoon salt

    Recipe:

    • Step 1

      • Boil 4 cups of water, add cornmeal and simmer for 30 min.

    • Step 2

      • Cook over medium heat, stirring frequently, until mixture thickens

    • Step 3

      • After cooking for hald an hour you can additionally put some butter on tip of the mush and let it rest for 10 minutes.

    • Step 4

      • Bon apetite!
